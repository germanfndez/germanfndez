# Hi, I'm Germán Fernández

I'm a software engineer from Uruguay, now based in San Francisco. I'm a **Founding Engineer at [Terac](https://terac.com)**, building infrastructure for the expert economy, and a **Puentes Fellow at Antigravity**.

I'm curious about software, companies, finance, and people. I learn by building things and paying attention to what happens around them.

## From game servers to AI products

I started building game servers when I was 12. That became a substantial part of my early work: at N93x Studios, I maintained 16 servers across multiple regions, with 120–140 players on each server.

From there, my work expanded into AI products. I co-founded **Realtic AI**, combining conversational AI and CRM workflows for real estate presales in Latin America. Multiple agencies used the product.

Later, at a stealth startup, I worked on an AI-native app builder with 300,000+ users. My work included observability, subagent orchestration, and model routing, reducing infrastructure and inference costs by up to 40%.

Today, I'm working on infrastructure for the expert economy at Terac. The projects below show other parts of that path, from game-server tooling to experiments with AI and music.
